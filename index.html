The Venue

Where the water meets the city, a new stage for Sydney’s most considered events.

Positioned on the waterfront at the centre of Darling Harbour’s convention and tourism precinct, Harbourside brings together six purpose-built spaces, a landscaped rooftop garden and more than 55 years of Doltone House event delivery.

  • 4,500sqm of flexible event space
  • 1,250 guests, subject to confirmed event configuration
  • 3,500sqm landscaped rooftop garden — Eloura Park
  • 6 event, pre-function and breakout spaces with green room and production facilities
  • 2027 opening on the Darling Harbour waterfront

The Architecture

Shaped by the meeting of water, sky and landscape.

From the waterfront promenade, arrival flows through layered sandstone and timber interiors toward the sculptural staircase, pre-function spaces and rooftop garden — a continuous journey from harbour to skyline.
